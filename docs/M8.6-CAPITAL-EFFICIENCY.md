# M8.6 Capital Efficiency

M8.6 adds position-aware decisions to the Turso-first data center.

## Main behavior

- Each hot-stock candidate can be `watch` or `holding`.
- Holding entries may store average cost, quantity, and purchase date.
- The UI calculates unrealized P/L, holding days, position health, capital efficiency, and an action recommendation.
- The summary table stays compact; detailed reasoning opens in a single-page modal.
- The modal closes through the top-right close control, the bottom Close button, backdrop click, or Escape. It never opens another browser window.
- Material advice changes are stored in `position_advice_events` for later model validation.

## Advice labels

`加碼`, `繼續持有`, `等待反彈`, `減碼`, `停損`, and `換股` are rule-based decision aids. They are not guaranteed forecasts or personalized financial advice.
